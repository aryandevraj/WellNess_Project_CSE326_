Safety and Moderation

Every report is reviewed by a WellNest moderator.

  1. You report an activity using the form below.
  2. A moderator reads the report and checks the listing.
  3. The listing is corrected, paused or removed if it breaks our guidelines.

Important safety disclaimer

WellNest is a community activity directory. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. WellNest cannot respond to emergencies. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

About WellNest

WellNest helps neighbours find local, non-clinical wellness activities such as walking groups, meditation, yoga, peer circles and gardening. Our goal is social connection, everyday movement and healthy routines.
